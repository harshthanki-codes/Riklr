# Fleet Manager

- Client: a Riyadh-based logistics group
- Location: Riyadh, with travel to Dammam and Jeddah
- Posted: February 2026

## About the role

You will run a fleet of about 600 heavy and light vehicles across the central region. You will report to the Head of Operations.

## What you will do

- Plan fleet capacity against monthly volume forecasts
- Manage maintenance contracts and keep vehicle uptime above 92%
- Run the fleet telematics system and act on driver behaviour data
- Control fleet cost per kilometre and fuel spend
- Lead a team of 4 supervisors and about 40 workshop staff

## What you need

- 10 or more years in fleet or transport operations, 5 in a management role
- Experience with fleet telematics and a maintenance management system
- Experience in Saudi Arabia
- Fluent Arabic and English
- Valid Saudi driving licence

## Package

- SAR 26,000 to 34,000 per month, plus housing and transport allowance
- Annual bonus

## Notes from intake

- Nationality: Saudi nationals only (confirmed with HR Business Partner)
- Interview panel: Head of Operations, HR Business Partner, Regional Director
