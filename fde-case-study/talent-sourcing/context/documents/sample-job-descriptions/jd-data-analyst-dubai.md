# Data Analyst

- Client: a Dubai e-commerce marketplace
- Location: Dubai, hybrid (3 days in office)
- Posted: June 2026

## About the role

You will join the commercial analytics team and support category managers with weekly performance reporting and ad hoc analysis.

## What you will do

- Build and maintain dashboards for sales, margin and stock
- Write SQL against the data warehouse
- Answer questions from category managers within 2 working days
- Help define metrics for new marketplace sellers

## What you need

- 2 to 4 years in a data or business analyst role
- Strong SQL and one BI tool
- Python is a plus
- English required. Arabic is a plus.
- UAE residence visa or able to relocate within 60 days

## Package

- AED 14,000 to 18,000 per month, all inclusive

## Notes from intake

- Nationality: Open. Emirati candidates welcome.
- Interview panel: Head of Commercial Analytics, one senior analyst
