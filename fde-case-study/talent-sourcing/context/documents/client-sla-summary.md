# Client service level summary

Summary of the standard terms in the firm's recruitment services agreement. Some clients have negotiated variations. These are listed at the end.

## What the firm commits to

| Commitment | Standard term |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge a new role | Within 1 working day |
| Hold the intake call | Within 2 working days of the role being raised |
| First shortlist | Within 10 working days of the role being confirmed. 7 working days for priority accounts. |
| Shortlist size | 3 to 6 candidates, each screened by phone |
| Replacement guarantee | Free replacement if a placed candidate leaves within 90 days |

## What the client commits to

| Commitment | Standard term |
|---|---|
| Intake | Make the hiring manager available for an intake call and share the salary range |
| Feedback on a shortlist | Within 3 working days of receiving it |
| Interview feedback | Within 2 working days of each interview |
| Changes to the role | Tell the firm in writing. A material change restarts the shortlist clock. |

## How the SLA is measured

- The clock starts when the role is confirmed. The agreement does not define "confirmed".
- Working days follow the client's country. Weekends are Saturday and Sunday in the UAE. They are Friday and Saturday in Saudi Arabia and Qatar.
- Public holidays are excluded
- The firm reports SLA performance to each client every quarter

## Fees

- 15% of first-year base salary for junior and mid roles
- 18% to 20% for senior roles
- Fee is due when the candidate joins

## Negotiated variations

| Client | Variation |
|---|---|
| Riyadh-based logistics group | 7 working day shortlist. 3 working day feedback removed from the contract at the client's request in 2024. |
| Dubai hospitality group | 10 working days for all roles, including senior |
| Riyadh retail bank | All shortlists to include at least 60% Saudi nationals unless agreed otherwise in writing |
