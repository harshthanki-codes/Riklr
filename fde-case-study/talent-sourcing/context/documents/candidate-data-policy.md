# Candidate data policy (summary)

Owner: Compliance and Data Officer. Approved by the Managing Partner in September 2025.

## Scope

- All candidate personal data held by the firm in the ATS, email, shared drive and messaging apps
- Applies to all three offices

## Rules

- Hold candidate data only with recorded consent. Consent covers the role applied for and, if ticked, future roles.
- A record with consent "N" may be used only for the role the candidate applied to
- A record with no consent recorded must not be used for new roles until consent is confirmed
- Keep a record for 24 months from the candidate's last activity. Activity means an application, a profile update or a consent confirmation.
- After 24 months, ask for fresh consent or delete the record
- Delete all copies of a candidate's data within 30 days of a request
- Do not send CVs to clients until the candidate has agreed to be put forward for that role
- Do not use photos, religion, marital status or health information in any screening decision
- Any new tool that processes candidate data needs approval from the Compliance and Data Officer before use

## Laws this policy follows

- UAE federal personal data protection law
- Saudi Personal Data Protection Law
- Qatar personal data privacy law

## Known gaps (as of October 2026)

- Automatic deletion after 24 months is not yet switched on in the ATS
- Records imported before 2023 often have no consent recorded
- The ATS does not update last activity when a recruiter attaches an existing record to a new role
