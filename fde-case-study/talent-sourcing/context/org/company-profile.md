# Company profile

## The company

- Talent consultancy founded in Dubai in 2012
- Recruits permanent staff for corporate clients across the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar
- Earns a fee of 15% to 20% of first-year salary when a candidate it placed joins
- Group revenue and ownership are not relevant to this case

## Offices

| Office | Opened | People | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dubai (head office) | 2012 | 18 recruiters and sourcers, plus leadership, compliance and finance | UAE clients and some regional roles |
| Riyadh | 2019 | 12 recruiters and sourcers | Saudi clients |
| Doha | 2022 | 5 recruiters and sourcers | Qatar clients |

## Systems

| System | Used for |
|---|---|
| ATS (cloud applicant tracking system) | Roles, candidate records, applications, stage changes. CV parsing for English CVs. |
| Two regional job boards | Job posts and inbound applications. One is strong in Saudi Arabia. |
| Professional network | Posts, inbound applications and outbound search |
| Client careers pages | Some clients forward applications from their own site |
| Internal candidate database | Candidates from past roles, stored in the ATS |
| Intake form | Word template filled during or after the intake call. Saved to the shared drive. |
| Spreadsheets | Each recruiter tracks their own roles. Head of Delivery keeps a weekly SLA sheet. |
| Email | Shortlists go to clients as email with CVs attached. Client feedback comes back by email. |
| Messaging app | Day-to-day chat with candidates and some hiring managers. Not an official record. |

## The role journey as documented

1. Hiring manager raises a role by email or phone
2. Recruiter holds the intake call and fills the intake form
3. Recruiter drafts the job description. Hiring manager approves it.
4. Role is posted on job boards, the professional network and the client careers page
5. Recruiter and sourcers source candidates from all channels and the internal database
6. Recruiter screens CVs, holds phone screens and runs assessments where needed
7. Shortlist goes to the client
8. Client gives feedback and interviews candidates
9. Offer, acceptance and onboarding support
